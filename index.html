Wesbury College of Science offers Grade 8–12 and Matric Upgrade programmes in a CAPS-aligned, English-medium environment — building confident, future-ready learners.

Since its establishment in 2007, Wesbury College of Science has consistently demonstrated academic excellence.

The school has achieved a 100% Matric pass rate with distinctions and was ranked among the top 5 schools in the Ugu District in its founding year. It has also been recognised among the top 20 schools in KwaZulu-Natal. More recently, the school maintained its strong academic performance with a 100% pass rate in the 2019 NSC examinations — reflecting its continued commitment to high educational standards.

Our Vision

The school's vision is to provide quality science education for all learners admitted, and to fully develop the potential of each learner not only scientifically but also socially, spiritually, and economically in a democratic environment.

Our Mission

The school's mission is to maintain a standard of achievement such that, in teaching and learning, it is judged to be one of the premier institutions in KwaZulu-Natal and the Republic of South Africa as a whole.

To achieve this, the school strives to admit learners from a cross-section of society, irrespective of their socio-economic background, ability, or previous attainments or failures, and who are able, with the support of its provision of thorough resources, to meet their goals.

The school does NOT DISCRIMINATE AGAINST ANY PERSON on the grounds of race, nationality, ethnic or national origin, religion, politics, or sex in implementing its Equal Opportunity Policy.

The school will incorporate the provisions in relation to age, disability, and sexual preferences within the law. The policy will be regularly reviewed, and arrangements made to monitor its application.
